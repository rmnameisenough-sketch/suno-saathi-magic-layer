How It Works

  • Gravitational force: F = Gm₁m₂/r²
  • Acceleration due to gravity: g = GM/r²
  • Weight: W = mg
  • Orbital velocity: v = √(GM/r)
  • Escape velocity: v = √(2GM/r)
  • Orbital period: T = 2πr/v

G = 6.67430 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²

M is the mass of the planet or central body, and r is distance from its centre.

Example

Calculate the weight of a 10 kg object on Earth using g = 9.8 m/s².

W = mg = 10 × 9.8 = 98 N

The object's weight is 98 N.

FAQs

1. What is gravitation?
The attractive force between objects having mass.

2. What is the SI unit of gravitational force?
Newton (N).

3. What is escape velocity?
The minimum speed required to escape a body's gravitational field without further propulsion, neglecting air resistance.

4. Does orbital velocity depend on satellite mass?
For an ideal circular orbit, orbital velocity does not depend on satellite mass.
