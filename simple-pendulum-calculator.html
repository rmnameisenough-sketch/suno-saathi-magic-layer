Physics Formula

T = 2π √(L / g)
  • T = Time period (seconds)
  • L = Pendulum length (metres)
  • g = Gravitational acceleration (m/s²)
  • f = Frequency (Hz)
f = 1 / T
L = gT² / 4π²
g = 4π²L / T²

Example

For a pendulum of length 1 m on Earth:

T = 2π √(1 / 9.81)

T ≈ 2.01 seconds

FAQs

Q. Does mass affect the time period?
No. For an ideal simple pendulum, time period does not depend on bob mass.

Q. Is this formula always exact?
No. It assumes small oscillation angles and negligible friction.

Q. What is the SI unit of time period?
Second (s).
