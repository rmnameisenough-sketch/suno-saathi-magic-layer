〰️ Oscillations and Waves: Formulas and Calculators

Oscillations repeat in time and waves repeat in time and space. Both rely on the same ideas of amplitude, period, frequency and angular frequency, so the calculators here share notation.

Wave speedv = fλm/s

x = A cos(ωt) assumes the motion starts at maximum displacement. The pendulum formula holds for small angles only (roughly under 15°).

Worked example

A simple pendulum is 1 m long. Find its time period and frequency (g = 9.8 m/s²).

  1. Time period: T = 2π√(L/g) = 2π√(1/9.8) ≈ 2 × 3.1416 × 0.3194 ≈ 2.007 s.
  2. Frequency: f = 1/T ≈ 0.498 Hz.

Try the same numbers in the calculators above to check each step.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using a large starting angle with the pendulum formula. It is only accurate for small swings.
  • Forgetting that the pendulum period does not depend on the mass of the bob.
  • Mixing frequency f (Hz) and angular frequency ω (rad/s).
  • Using centimetres for length or extension while using SI units for the other values.

Quick questions

Does amplitude change the period in SHM?

For ideal simple harmonic motion, no. The period depends only on the mass and spring constant (or on length and g for a pendulum).

How are wave speed, frequency and wavelength related?

v = fλ. For the same medium, a higher frequency means a shorter wavelength.
