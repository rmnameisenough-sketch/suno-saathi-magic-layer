How It Works

  • Torque: τ = rF sinθ
  • Angular acceleration: α = τ/I
  • Moment of inertia: I = τ/α
  • Angular momentum: L = Iω
  • Rotational kinetic energy: KE = ½Iω²
  • Angular velocity: ω = θ/t
  • Angular acceleration: α = Δω/t

Here, r is perpendicular distance from the axis, F is force, θ is angle between r and F, I is moment of inertia, and ω is angular velocity.

Example

A force of 10 N acts perpendicular to a rod at a distance of 2 m from its pivot.

τ = rF sin90° = 2 × 10 × 1 = 20 N·m

The torque is 20 N·m.

FAQs

1. What is torque?
Torque is the turning effect of force about an axis.

2. What is the SI unit of torque?
Newton metre (N·m).

3. What is moment of inertia?
It measures resistance to angular acceleration.

4. What is angular momentum?
Angular momentum is rotational momentum, represented by L.
