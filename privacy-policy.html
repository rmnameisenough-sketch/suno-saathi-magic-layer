Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to Suno Saathi Tools. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information may be handled when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

Our calculators are designed to work without requiring an account or registration. You can use our tools without submitting personal information.

If you contact us through email or a contact form, we may receive information such as your name, email address and message.

2. How We Use Information

Information received through communication may be used to:

  • Respond to your questions and feedback.
  • Improve website functionality and user experience.
  • Address technical problems or support requests.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

To make the site more useful, some features save small pieces of information in your own browser. This includes your light or dark theme choice, a short list of your recent calculations (the calculator name, the values you entered and the result), and saved copies of pages so they can open without an internet connection.

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can remove the recent calculations with the "Clear history" button on any calculator page, or clear all saved site data in your browser settings.

If you use the Share button, the values you entered are included in the link you choose to share. Avoid sharing links that contain anything you consider private.

4. Cookies, Analytics and Advertising

At the time of writing, this website does not display advertising, does not use analytics tools, and does not set cookies of its own.

Instead of cookies, some features use your browser's local storage to remember your light or dark theme choice and your recent calculations. The site may also keep saved copies of its pages in your browser (through a service worker) so that they can open offline. This information stays on your device and is described in section 3. You can remove it at any time in your browser settings.

Like most websites, this site is delivered by a hosting provider, which may keep standard server logs.

If we add advertising (for example Google AdSense) or analytics in the future, those services may use cookies or similar technologies, for example to show ads and measure how they perform. Before that happens we will update this policy, and where the law requires it we will ask for your consent.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or use third-party services. These services have their own privacy policies and practices. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain website security. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Our tools may be useful to students and learners. We do not intentionally request personal information from children through our calculators. If you believe personal information has been submitted improperly, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
