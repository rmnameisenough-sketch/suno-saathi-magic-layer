FAQs

1. What is SHM?
Periodic motion where restoring force is proportional to displacement and directed toward equilibrium.

2. What is angular frequency?
Angular rate of oscillation, measured in rad/s.

3. What is the spring time period?
T = 2π√(m/k).

4. What is the pendulum time period?
For small oscillations, T = 2π√(L/g).
