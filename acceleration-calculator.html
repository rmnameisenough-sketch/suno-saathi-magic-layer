Formula Used

a = (v − u) ÷ t
v = u + at
u = v − at
t = (v − u) ÷ a

These equations apply when acceleration is constant.

Example

A car's velocity changes from 5 m/s to 25 m/s in 4 seconds.

a = (25 − 5) ÷ 4 = 5 m/s²

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is acceleration?
Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity per unit time.

2. What is its SI unit?
Meter per second squared (m/s²).

3. Can acceleration be negative?
Yes. Negative acceleration can indicate acceleration opposite to the chosen positive direction.

4. What is uniform acceleration?
Acceleration that remains constant over time.
