Formula Used

Density: ρ = m ÷ V

Mass: m = ρ × V

Volume: V = m ÷ ρ

Density SI unit: kg/m³ | Mass: kg | Volume: m³

How It Works

  • Density: Mass contained per unit volume.
  • Mass: Amount of matter in an object.
  • Volume: Space occupied by an object.

Density is commonly expressed in kg/m³ or g/cm³.

1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³.

Examples

Density: Mass = 2 kg, Volume = 0.001 m³. Density = 2000 kg/m³.

Mass: Density = 1000 kg/m³, Volume = 0.002 m³. Mass = 2 kg.

Volume: Mass = 5 kg, Density = 2500 kg/m³. Volume = 0.002 m³.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is density?

Density is the mass of a substance divided by its volume.

What is the SI unit of density?

The SI unit is kilogram per cubic metre (kg/m³).

Can density be measured in g/cm³?

Yes. g/cm³ is a commonly used density unit. 1 g/cm³ equals 1000 kg/m³.

Does density depend on volume?

For a uniform substance under the same conditions, density remains the same even when the quantity changes.
