x = F/k

5. FAQs

What is Hooke's law?

Within the elastic limit, the restoring force of a spring is proportional to its displacement from equilibrium.

What is spring constant?

It measures the stiffness of a spring. A larger k means a stiffer spring.

What is the unit of k?

Newton per metre (N/m).

Is Hooke's law always valid?

No. It is valid only within the spring's elastic limit.
