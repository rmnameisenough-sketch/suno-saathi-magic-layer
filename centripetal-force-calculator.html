2. Circular Motion Formulas

a = v² / r

Centripetal acceleration directed toward the centre.

F = mv² / r

Centripetal force required for circular motion.

v = √(ar)

Velocity from acceleration and radius.

r = v² / a

Radius from velocity and acceleration.

3. Example

A 2 kg object moves at 5 m/s in a circle of radius 10 m.

F = mv²/r

5. FAQs

What is centripetal force?

It is the net inward force required to keep an object moving along a circular path.

What is centripetal acceleration?

It is acceleration directed toward the centre of the circular path.

Is centripetal force a separate force?

No. It is the name for the net inward force provided by forces such as tension, friction or gravity.

Does velocity change in circular motion?

In uniform circular motion, speed stays constant but velocity changes direction.
