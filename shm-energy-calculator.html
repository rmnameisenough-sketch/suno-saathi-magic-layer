5. FAQs

What is SHM?

Simple harmonic motion is periodic motion in which restoring force is proportional to displacement and directed toward equilibrium.

Where is kinetic energy maximum?

Kinetic energy is maximum at the equilibrium position.

Where is potential energy maximum?

Spring potential energy is maximum at the extreme positions.

Is total energy constant?

In ideal SHM without damping, total mechanical energy remains constant.
