How It Works

  • Hydrostatic pressure: P = ρgh
  • Buoyant force: Fb = ρgV
  • Density: ρ = m/V
  • Volume flow rate: Q = Av
  • Flow velocity: v = Q/A
  • Continuity equation: A₁v₁ = A₂v₂
  • Bernoulli pressure difference: ΔP = ½ρ(v₂² − v₁²)

ρ is fluid density, g is gravitational acceleration, h is depth, A is cross-sectional area and V is displaced fluid volume.

Example

Water has density 1000 kg/m³. Find pressure at depth 2 m, using g = 9.8 m/s².

P = ρgh = 1000 × 9.8 × 2 = 19600 Pa

The gauge pressure is 19,600 Pa.

FAQs

1. What is fluid pressure?
Force exerted by a fluid per unit area.

2. What is buoyancy?
The upward force exerted by a fluid on an immersed object.

3. What is the SI unit of flow rate?
m³/s.

4. What is the continuity equation?
For steady incompressible flow, the volume flow rate remains constant.
