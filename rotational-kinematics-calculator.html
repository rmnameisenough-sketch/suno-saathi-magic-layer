Rotational Kinematics Formulas

ω = ω₀ + αt
θ = ω₀t + ½αt²
ω² = ω₀² + 2αθ
  • ω₀ = Initial angular velocity
  • ω = Final angular velocity
  • α = Angular acceleration
  • θ = Angular displacement
  • t = Time

These equations assume constant angular acceleration.

Example

Initial angular velocity = 2 rad/s, angular acceleration = 3 rad/s², time = 4 s.

θ = 2 × 4 + ½ × 3 × 4²

Angular displacement = 32 rad

FAQs

Q. What is angular velocity?
It is the rate of change of angular displacement.

Q. What is angular acceleration?
It is the rate of change of angular velocity.

Q. What is the SI unit of angular displacement?
Radian (rad).
