Centre of Mass Calculator

Calculate the centre of mass of particles placed along a straight line (1D).

Illustration

Physics Formula

xCOM = Σ(mx) / Σm
  • m = Mass of particle (kg)
  • x = Position of particle (m)
  • xCOM = Centre of mass position (m)
  • Σ = Sum of all particles

This calculator assumes particles lie on a single straight line.

Example

Particle 1: mass = 2 kg, position = 0 m

Particle 2: mass = 4 kg, position = 3 m

xCOM = (2×0 + 4×3)/(2+4)

xCOM = 2 m

How It Works

  • Select number of particles.
  • Enter mass and position of each particle.
  • Click Calculate.
  • The calculator finds the weighted average position.

FAQs

Q. What is centre of mass?
It is the mass-weighted average position of a system.

Q. Can the COM lie outside the particles?
For positive masses on a line, it lies between the extreme positions.

Q. What is the SI unit?
Metre (m).
