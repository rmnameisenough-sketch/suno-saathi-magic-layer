Moment of Inertia Calculator

Calculate moment of inertia and rotational quantities for a point mass.

Illustration

Point mass rotating at a distance r from the axis.

Physics Formulas

I = mr²
K = ½ Iω²
L = Iω
τ = Iα
  • I = Moment of inertia (kg·m²)
  • m = Mass (kg)
  • r = Distance from rotational axis (m)
  • ω = Angular velocity (rad/s)
  • α = Angular acceleration (rad/s²)
  • K = Rotational kinetic energy (J)
  • L = Angular momentum (kg·m²/s)
  • τ = Torque (N·m)

This calculator uses the point-mass model I = mr². Extended objects require their appropriate moment-of-inertia formula.

Example

Mass = 2 kg and distance from axis = 0.5 m.

I = 2 × (0.5)²

I = 0.5 kg·m²

How It Works

  • Select the quantity to calculate.
  • Enter mass and distance from axis.
  • For energy, angular momentum or torque, enter the rotational quantity requested.
  • Click Calculate to view the answer.

FAQs

Q. What is moment of inertia?
It measures an object's resistance to angular acceleration about an axis.

Q. Does mass distribution matter?
Yes. Moment of inertia depends on how mass is distributed relative to the axis.

Q. What is its SI unit?
kg·m².
