Friction Force Calculator

Calculate the force opposing relative motion between surfaces.

1. Select Calculation

SI units: force in newtons (N), mass in kg, coefficient of friction is dimensionless.

2. Friction Formulas

F = μN

Friction force = coefficient × normal force.

μ = F / N

Coefficient of friction.

N = F / μ

Normal force when friction and coefficient are known.

N = mg

Normal force on a horizontal surface when no other vertical forces act.

3. Example

A block has normal force 50 N and coefficient of friction 0.4.

F = μN

F = 0.4 × 50

Friction force = 20 N

5. FAQs

What is friction?

Friction is a force that opposes relative motion or the tendency of relative motion between surfaces.

What is μ?

μ represents the coefficient of friction between two surfaces.

Does friction always equal μN?

For kinetic friction, F = μkN. For static friction, friction adjusts up to a maximum μsN.

What is normal force?

Normal force is the perpendicular contact force exerted by a surface.
