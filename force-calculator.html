Formula Used

F = m × a
m = F ÷ a
a = F ÷ m

Newton's Second Law states that net force equals mass multiplied by acceleration.

Example

A 10 kg object accelerates at 3 m/s².

F = 10 × 3 = 30 N

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is force?
Force is an interaction that can change an object's motion.

2. What is the SI unit of force?
Newton (N).

3. What is one Newton?
One Newton is the force needed to accelerate 1 kg by 1 m/s².

4. Is mass the same as weight?
No. Mass is measured in kilograms, while weight is a force measured in Newtons.
