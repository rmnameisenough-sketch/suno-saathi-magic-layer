How It Works

For an object moving in a circular path, its velocity direction continuously changes. Centripetal acceleration acts towards the centre of the circle.

  • Acceleration: a = v²/r
  • Force: F = mv²/r
  • Angular velocity: ω = v/r
  • Time period: T = 2πr/v
  • Frequency: f = v/(2πr)
  • Speed: v = ωr

Example

A car moves at 10 m/s around a circular track of radius 5 m.

a = v²/r = 10²/5 = 20 m/s²

The centripetal acceleration is 20 m/s².

FAQs

1. What is centripetal acceleration?
Acceleration directed towards the centre of a circular path.

2. What is the SI unit of centripetal force?
Newton (N).

3. What is angular velocity?
Angular displacement per unit time, measured in rad/s.

4. What is the relation between period and frequency?
f = 1/T.
