💡 Electricity and Optics: Formulas and Calculators

Electricity problems mostly use three relations, V = IR and the power and energy formulas. Optics adds the sign convention, which is where most marks are lost.

Key formulas

What you findFormulaUnit
Ohm’s lawV = IRV
Electrical powerP = VIW
Power using current and resistanceP = I²RW
Power using voltage and resistanceP = V² / RW
Electrical energyE = PtJ
Energy in kilowatt-hourskWh = kW × hourskWh
Lens formula (Cartesian)1/f = 1/v − 1/ucm⁻¹
Mirror formula (Cartesian)1/f = 1/v + 1/ucm⁻¹
Magnification from heightsm = hᵢ / hₒ–
Magnification, mirrorm = −v / u–
Magnification, lensm = v / u–

1 kWh = 3,600,000 J. In the Cartesian convention, distances are measured from the lens or mirror, with the direction of incoming light positive, so a real object has a negative u.

Worked example

A 4 Ω resistor is connected to a 12 V supply for 2 hours. Find the current, power and energy used. Then find the image distance for a convex lens (f = +10 cm) with a real object 30 cm away.

  1. Current: I = V/R = 12 / 4 = 3 A.
  2. Power: P = VI = 12 × 3 = 36 W. Energy: 36 W × 2 h = 72 Wh = 0.072 kWh.
  3. Lens: use u = −30 cm and f = +10 cm in 1/v = 1/f + 1/u. 1/v = 1/10 − 1/30 = 1/15, so v = +15 cm (a real image on the far side).

Try the same numbers in the calculators above to check each step.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Entering the object distance as a positive number in the Cartesian convention. A real object has a negative u.
  • Mixing watts and kilowatts when finding energy in kWh.
  • Forgetting that a concave mirror has a negative focal length in the Cartesian convention.
  • Using the lens magnification formula for a mirror, or the reverse.

Quick questions

What is one unit of electricity?

One unit is 1 kilowatt-hour, which is 3.6 million joules.

What does a negative magnification mean?

The image is inverted relative to the object.
