Calculate Pressure, Force, Area and Liquid Pressure easily.

Formula

P = F / A

Pressure is force acting per unit area.

How It Works

  1. Select what you want to calculate.
  2. Enter the required SI values.
  3. Click Calculate.
  4. View the result and calculation steps.

Example

Force = 100 N and Area = 2 m²

P = 100 / 2 = 50 Pa

FAQs

1. What is pressure?
Pressure is force per unit area.

2. What is the SI unit of pressure?
Pascal (Pa).

3. What is 1 Pascal?
1 Pa = 1 N/m².

4. What is liquid pressure?
Pressure due to liquid depth, density and gravity.
