Formulas Used

Energy: E = Pt

Energy: E = VIt

Power: P = E ÷ t

Time: t = E ÷ P

Energy: Joule (J) | Power: Watt (W) | Time: second (s)

How It Works

  • Electrical Energy: Energy transferred by an electrical device.
  • Power: Rate at which energy is consumed or transferred.
  • Time: Duration for which the device operates.
  • kWh: Common unit used in electricity bills.

1 kWh = 3,600,000 J.

Examples

Energy: A 100 W device operates for 10 seconds. E = 100 × 10 = 1000 J.

Energy: A 220 V device draws 2 A for 5 seconds. E = 220 × 2 × 5 = 2200 J.

Power: 600 J energy is used in 20 seconds. P = 600 ÷ 20 = 30 W.

Time: A 100 W device uses 500 J. t = 500 ÷ 100 = 5 seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is electrical energy?

Electrical energy is energy transferred by electric charges through an electrical circuit.

What is the difference between kW and kWh?

kW measures power. kWh measures energy consumed over time.

How is electricity consumption calculated?

Energy in kWh = Power in kW × Time in hours.

What is one unit of electricity?

One unit of electricity is 1 kWh, equivalent to 3.6 million joules.
