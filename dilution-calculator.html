Formula Used

M₁V₁ = M₂V₂

In dilution, the amount of solute remains constant. Adding solvent decreases concentration but increases total solution volume.

Important: Concentration should use the same unit on both sides. This calculator uses mol/L (M), and volume in mL.

Example

Prepare 400 mL of 0.5 M solution from a 2 M stock solution.

M₂0.5 M

V₁100 mL

Answer: Take 100 mL stock solution and add solvent until the final total volume reaches 400 mL.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is dilution?
Dilution is reducing solution concentration by adding solvent.

2. What is M₁V₁ = M₂V₂?
It is the dilution equation. The quantity of solute remains constant before and after dilution.

3. Is stock volume the final volume?
No. Stock volume is the amount taken from the concentrated solution. Final volume includes stock plus added solvent.

4. Can I dilute a weaker solution into a stronger one?
Ordinary dilution cannot increase concentration. To obtain a more concentrated solution, a different preparation method is needed.
