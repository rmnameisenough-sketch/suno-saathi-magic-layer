Rolling Motion Formulas

v = ωR
s = Rθ
K = ½mv² + ½Iω²
  • v = Linear velocity (m/s)
  • ω = Angular velocity (rad/s)
  • R = Radius (m)
  • s = Distance travelled (m)
  • θ = Angular displacement (rad)
  • m = Mass (kg)
  • I = Moment of inertia (kg·m²)

For rolling without slipping, the contact point has zero instantaneous velocity relative to the ground.

Example

Wheel radius = 0.5 m and linear velocity = 5 m/s.

ω = v/R = 5/0.5

Angular velocity = 10 rad/s

FAQs

Q. What is rolling without slipping?
It is motion where the surface contact does not slide relative to the ground.

Q. What is the relation between v and ω?
For rolling without slipping, v = ωR.

Q. Does rolling object have rotational energy?
Yes. It has both translational and rotational kinetic energy.
