Heat & Temperature Calculator

Calculate heat energy and convert temperature units.

Calculator

Formula

Q = mcΔT

Q = heat energy, m = mass, c = specific heat capacity, ΔT = temperature change.

How It Works

  1. Select the quantity you want to calculate.
  2. Enter the required values.
  3. Use SI units for accurate calculations.
  4. Click Calculate to see the result and steps.

Example

Mass = 2 kg, Specific heat = 4200 J/kg·K, Temperature change = 5 K

Q = 2 × 4200 × 5 = 42,000 J

FAQs

1. What is heat energy?
Energy transferred because of a temperature difference.

2. What is the SI unit of heat?
Joule (J).

3. What is specific heat capacity?
Heat required to raise 1 kg of a substance by 1 K.

4. Is temperature change in Celsius equal to Kelvin?
Yes, a change of 1°C equals a change of 1 K.
