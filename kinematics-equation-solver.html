3. Example

A vehicle starts at 5 m/s and accelerates at 2 m/s² for 4 seconds.

v = u + at

v = 5 + (2 × 4)

Final velocity = 13 m/s

5. FAQs

What is kinematics?

Kinematics describes motion without studying the forces causing it.

What is u?

u represents initial velocity.

What is v?

v represents final velocity.

When are these equations valid?

These equations are valid for constant acceleration in one dimension.
