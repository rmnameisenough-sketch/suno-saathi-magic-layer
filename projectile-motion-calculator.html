Projectile Motion Calculator

Calculate projectile range, height and flight time.

Calculator

Formula

R = u² sin(2θ) / g

Assumes projectile lands at the same height from which it was launched and air resistance is neglected.

How It Works

  1. Choose the required projectile quantity.
  2. Enter velocity, angle and gravity.
  3. Use degrees for launch angle.
  4. Click Calculate to see the result and steps.

Example

Initial velocity = 20 m/s, angle = 45°, gravity = 9.8 m/s²

R = 400 × sin(90°) / 9.8 ≈ 40.82 m

FAQs

1. What is projectile motion?
Motion of an object launched into the air under gravity.

2. What is the maximum range angle?
45° for equal launch and landing heights, without air resistance.

3. What is time of flight?
Total time the projectile remains in the air.

4. Does air resistance matter?
This calculator ignores air resistance.
