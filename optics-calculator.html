Optics Calculator

Calculate image distance, focal length and magnification.

Calculator

Formula

Lens: 1/f = 1/v − 1/u
Mirror: 1/f = 1/v + 1/u

This calculator uses the Cartesian sign convention, so signs matter. Measure distances from the lens or mirror, with the direction of incoming light as positive. For a real object, enter the object distance u as a negative number.

Example: a convex lens has f = +10 cm and a real object is 30 cm in front of it. Enter u = −30 and f = 10 to get v = +15 cm (a real image on the other side). For a concave mirror, f is negative: u = −30 and f = −15 gives v = −30 cm (a real image in front of the mirror).

How It Works

  1. Select lens, mirror or magnification calculation.
  2. Enter the known values.
  3. Use consistent units (cm in this calculator).
  4. Click Calculate to get the answer and steps.

Examples

m = -2/4 = -0.5

FAQs

1. What is focal length?
Distance between optical center/pole and principal focus.

2. What is magnification?
Ratio of image height to object height.

3. What does negative magnification mean?
Image is inverted under the chosen convention.

4. Which sign convention is used?
Cartesian sign convention.
