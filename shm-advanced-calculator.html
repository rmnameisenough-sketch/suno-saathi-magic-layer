How It Works

  • Angular frequency: ω = 2π/T
  • Time period: T = 2π/ω
  • Displacement: x = A cos(ωt)
  • Velocity: v = −Aω sin(ωt)
  • Acceleration: a = −ω²x
  • Maximum velocity: vmax = Aω
  • Maximum acceleration: amax = Aω²
  • Spring time period: T = 2π√(m/k)
  • Total energy: E = ½mω²A²

Example

An oscillator has amplitude 0.1 m and angular frequency 5 rad/s.

vmax = Aω = 0.1 × 5 = 0.5 m/s

Its maximum velocity is 0.5 m/s.

FAQs

1. What is SHM?
Oscillatory motion in which restoring acceleration is proportional to displacement and directed towards the mean position.

2. What is amplitude?
The maximum displacement from the equilibrium position.

3. What is angular frequency?
The rate of change of phase, measured in rad/s.

4. What is the SI unit of spring constant?
Newton per metre (N/m).
