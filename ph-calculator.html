Formula Used

pH = −log₁₀[H⁺]
pOH = −log₁₀[OH⁻]
pH + pOH = 14
[H⁺] = 10⁻ᵖᴴ
[OH⁻] = 10⁻ᵖᴼᴴ

At 25°C, pH + pOH = 14 for aqueous solutions.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is pH?
pH indicates the acidity or basicity of an aqueous solution.

2. What is pOH?
pOH represents hydroxide ion concentration on a logarithmic scale.

3. What is neutral pH?
At 25°C, neutral aqueous solutions have pH 7.

4. Is pH always between 0 and 14?
No. Concentrated solutions can have pH below 0 or above 14.

5. Is pH + pOH always 14?
This relation is approximately 14 at 25°C. It changes with temperature.
