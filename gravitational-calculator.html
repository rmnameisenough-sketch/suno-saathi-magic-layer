Formulas Used

Weight: W = mg

Mass: m = W ÷ g

Gravity: g = W ÷ m

Gravitational Force: F = Gm₁m₂ ÷ r²

G = 6.67430 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²

How It Works

  • Weight: Gravitational force acting on an object.
  • Mass: Amount of matter in an object.
  • Gravity: Gravitational acceleration at a location.
  • Gravitational Force: Attraction between two masses.

Earth's approximate gravitational acceleration is 9.8 m/s².

Examples

Weight: Mass = 10 kg, g = 9.8 m/s². W = 98 N.

Mass: Weight = 98 N, g = 9.8 m/s². m = 10 kg.

Gravity: Weight = 196 N, mass = 20 kg. g = 9.8 m/s².

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between mass and weight?

Mass measures matter and is measured in kilograms. Weight is gravitational force and is measured in newtons.

Does weight change on the Moon?

Yes. Weight changes with gravitational acceleration, but mass remains the same.

What is the value of gravity on Earth?

Approximately 9.8 m/s² near Earth's surface.

What is universal gravitational constant?

G is approximately 6.67430 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg².
