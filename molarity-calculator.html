📘 How It Works

Molarity describes the concentration of a solution. It is the number of moles of dissolved solute per liter of solution.

Molarity (M) = Moles ÷ Volume (L)

Moles = Molarity × Volume (L)

Grams = Moles × Molar Mass

Example: 0.5 mol NaCl in 500 mL

  • Volume = 500 ÷ 1000 = 0.5 L
  • Molarity = 0.5 ÷ 0.5 = 1 M
  • Solution concentration = 1 mol/L

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is molarity?

Molarity is the number of moles of solute per liter of solution.

What is the unit of molarity?

Molarity is expressed as mol/L or M.

Can I calculate molarity from grams?

Yes. Enter the chemical formula, mass in grams and solution volume.

Does volume mean solvent volume?
